# AI-VOS - Start Here

Version: 1.1.0

---

## What is AI-VOS?

AI-VOS is an AI operating system that helps you turn a raw idea
into a revenue-generating business.

You do NOT need programming knowledge.
AI does the thinking. You do the deciding and executing.

---

## How It Works

AI-VOS guides you through 8 phases:

| Phase | What Happens | You Need To |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 0 | Explore your idea | Describe your idea to AI |
| 1 | Validate the market | Answer AI questions |
| 2 | Design revenue model | Approve pricing strategy |
| 3 | Define the product | Approve features and MVP |
| 4 | Plan the software | Approve technical decisions |
| 5 | Build the product | Run commands AI gives you |
| 6 | Launch | Execute marketing plan |
| 7 | Grow | Review and improve |

---

## How to Start a Session

1. Open your AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.).
2. Give it access to this repository.
3. Tell the AI: Read boot/SYSTEM.yaml and follow the boot sequence.
4. The AI will read the required files automatically.
5. The AI will ask you questions. Answer them.
6. The AI will give you commands to run. Copy and paste them.
7. Approve changes when the AI asks.

---

## Your Role (Human Operator)

- Describe your idea or problem.
- Answer AI questions honestly.
- Run terminal commands the AI provides.
- Approve or reject AI recommendations.
- Provide business context and domain knowledge.

## AI Role

- Analyze your idea and market.
- Design business model and pricing.
- Define product features.
- Plan and guide software development.
- Create marketing and launch strategy.
- Provide copy-paste ready commands.

---

## Important Rules

- The repository is the memory. Everything important is saved there.
- Chat history is temporary. Do not rely on it.
- No change happens without your approval.
- AI will never assume. It will ask you.
