# Revenue Model Engine - Outputs

## Primary Outputs

| Output | File | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Business Model | business/BUSINESS-MODEL.md | Complete revenue and pricing strategy. |

## Document Contents

1. Revenue Stream Overview
2. Pricing Strategy (tiers, plans, subscription vs one-time)
3. Cost Structure (fixed, variable, margins)
4. 12-Month Financial Projection
5. Break-Even Analysis
6. Payment Infrastructure Recommendation
7. Upsell and Cross-sell Strategy
8. Key Financial Assumptions
9. Revenue Growth Scenarios (conservative, moderate, optimistic)

## Handoff

After approval, outputs go to Product Design Engine and Go-To-Market Engine.
