# Go-To-Market Engine - Outputs

## Primary Outputs

| Output | File | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| GTM Strategy | business/GO-TO-MARKET.md | Complete launch and growth strategy. |
| Deployment Plan | deployment/DEPLOYMENT.md | Technical and operational launch plan. |

## Document Contents

### Go-To-Market Strategy includes:
1. Marketing Strategy Overview
2. Target Channel Analysis (organic, paid, partnerships)
3. Customer Acquisition Funnel (awareness to conversion)
4. Content Marketing Plan (topics, frequency, platforms)
5. Launch Timeline and Checklist
6. KPI Dashboard (metrics, targets, tracking)
7. Growth Strategy (retention, referral, expansion)
8. Marketing Tools Recommendation
9. Budget Allocation Plan
10. Risk Mitigation for Launch

## Handoff

After approval, deployment plan goes to technical engines.
Marketing plan is ready for human operator execution.
