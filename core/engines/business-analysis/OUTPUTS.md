# Business Analysis Engine - Outputs

## Primary Outputs

| Output | File | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Opportunity Report | business/BUSINESS-OPPORTUNITY.md | Complete business analysis. |
| Market Validation | business/MARKET-VALIDATION.md | Market demand evidence. |

## Report Contents

1. Executive Summary
2. Problem Definition
3. Target Market Analysis (TAM, SAM, SOM)
4. Competitor Analysis
5. Problem-Solution Fit Assessment
6. SWOT Analysis
7. Risk and Assumption Register
8. Go / No-Go Recommendation
9. Open Questions

## Handoff

After approval, outputs go to Revenue Model Engine and Product Design Engine.
