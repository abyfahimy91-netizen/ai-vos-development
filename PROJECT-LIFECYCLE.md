# AI-VOS Project Lifecycle

## Purpose

This document defines the standard lifecycle for creating, developing and operating any business project managed by AI-VOS.

The AI must follow phases in order and must not skip critical validation steps.

---

# Phase 1 - Idea Discovery

Goal:
Understand the business idea.

AI Tasks:
- Receive initial idea.
- Define problem.
- Identify potential customers.
- Identify assumptions.

Required Output:
Business concept document.

Human Confirmation:
Required before moving forward.

---

# Phase 2 - Market Validation

Goal:
Evaluate whether the business opportunity exists.

AI Tasks:
- Analyze market.
- Identify competitors.
- Define customer segments.
- Test assumptions.

Required Output:
Market validation report.

Human Confirmation:
Required.

---

# Phase 3 - Business Model Design

Goal:
Create a sustainable revenue model.

AI Tasks:
- Define value proposition.
- Define pricing.
- Define revenue streams.
- Estimate costs.

Required Output:
Business model document.

---

# Phase 4 - Product Planning

Goal:
Design the product.

AI Tasks:
- Create PRD.
- Define MVP.
- Prioritize features.

Required Output:
Product requirements document.

---

# Phase 5 - Technology Planning

Goal:
Select technical architecture.

AI Tasks:
- Select programming languages.
- Select database.
- Design architecture.
- Define infrastructure.

Rules:
AI must not choose technology without considering project requirements.

---

# Phase 6 - Development

Goal:
Build the product.

AI Tasks:
- Generate code.
- Explain commands.
- Wait for human execution.
- Review results.

Rules:
No irreversible changes without confirmation.

---

# Phase 7 - Launch

Goal:
Deploy and make available.

AI Tasks:
- Prepare deployment plan.
- Prepare monitoring.
- Validate functionality.

---

# Phase 8 - Growth and Operation

Goal:
Operate and improve the business.

AI Tasks:
- Marketing analysis.
- Revenue optimization.
- Product improvement.
- Operational planning.

---

## General Rules

- Repository is the source of truth.
- Chat history is not permanent memory.
- AI must work step by step.
- Human executor performs external actions.
- AI must explain commands and expected results.
- AI must wait for confirmation after operational steps.
